# D-082: the most played gaps of the EDHREC top 1,000

Comprehensive Rules effective 2026-11-06, quoted verbatim. Tests:
`crates/harness/tests/forge_top1000.rs`.

## Changing targets (115.7)

> 115.3. The same target can’t be chosen multiple times for any one instance of the word
> “target” on a spell or ability. If the spell or ability uses the word “target” in multiple
> places, the same object or player can be chosen once for each instance of the word
> “target” (as long as it fits the targeting criteria). This rule applies both when choosing
> targets for a spell or ability and when changing targets or choosing new targets for a
> spell or ability (see rule 115.7).

> 115.5. A spell or ability on the stack is an illegal target for itself.

> 115.7. Some effects allow a player to change the target(s) of a spell or ability, and other
> effects allow a player to choose new targets for a spell or ability.

> 115.7a If an effect allows a player to “change the target(s)” of a spell or ability, each
> target can be changed only to another legal target. If a target can’t be changed to
> another legal target, the original target is unchanged, even if the original target is
> itself illegal by then. If all the targets aren’t changed to other legal targets, none of
> them are changed.

> 115.7b If an effect allows a player to “change a target” of a spell or ability, the process
> described in rule 115.7a is followed, except that only one of those targets may be changed
> (rather than all of them or none of them).

> 115.7c If an effect allows a player to “change any targets” of a spell or ability, the
> process described in rule 115.7a is followed, except that any number of those targets may
> be changed (rather than all of them or none of them).

> 115.7d If an effect allows a player to “choose new targets” for a spell or ability, the
> player may leave any number of the targets unchanged, even if those targets would be
> illegal. If the player chooses to change some or all of the targets, the new targets must
> be legal and must not cause any unchanged targets to become illegal.

> 115.7e When changing targets or choosing new targets for a spell or ability, only the
> final set of targets is evaluated to determine whether the change is legal.

> 115.7f A spell or ability may “divide” or “distribute” an effect (such as damage or
> counters) among one or more targets. When changing targets or choosing new targets for
> that spell or ability, the original division can’t be changed.

In the engine (`Effect::ChangeTargets`, `Work::ChangeTargets`): the controller of the
changing spell goes through the targets slot by slot (`ChoiceKind::NewTarget`). "Choose new
targets" (115.7d) offers the current target first (keep) and every legal one; "change the
target" of a spell with a single target (115.7a with one target) offers only other legal
targets, and with none the target stays. Legal is as for the changed spell or ability and its
controller (`targeting::candidates` with its filter, CR 115.5 included), and a slot doesn't
offer a target another slot of the same instance of "target" has (115.3). The new set is
written at once (`GameAction::SetTargets`, `Event::TargetsChanged`); modes, X and the
spell's other choices stay. Not built: 115.7b/c ("change a target", "any targets"), a fixed
new target (Spellskite), random targets, a restriction on the new target, divided effects
(none compiles yet), "becomes the target" triggers (none exist yet; when they come they must
fire for the changed targets here, as Forge's ChangeTargetsEffect does).

## Everything of a kind from graveyards and hands (400.3, 110.2a, 701.24a)

> 400.3. If an object would go to any library, graveyard, or hand other than its owner’s, it
> goes to its owner’s corresponding zone.

> 110.2a If an effect instructs a player to put an object onto the battlefield, that object
> enters the battlefield under that player’s control unless the effect states otherwise.

> 701.24a To shuffle a library or a face-down pile of cards, randomize the cards within it so
> that no player knows their order.

Forge's `ChangeZoneAll` from graveyards or hands (`Effect::MoveAll`): every player's zone
unless `Defined$` or a target player says whose; a filter of "your" (`YouCtrl`/`YouOwn`) cards
is your zone only — and only you shuffle (Forge would shuffle every library). Onto the
battlefield a card enters under its owner's control, under yours with `GainControl$` ("under
your control"); other players' cards need the card to say whose control (110.2a), else the
card is blocked. Into a library only shuffled (the owners would order them, 401.4).
Manabond is in `SCRIPT_DIFFERS` (its script reveals nothing).

`ChangeZone:no-target` is now named by its shape (`… Graveyard->Battlefield this card`,
`… Defined TriggeredNewCardLKICopy`, `… Command->Hand ChangeType …`): most need triggers
that work from a graveyard, which aren't built.
